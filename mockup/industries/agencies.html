Can my team work on the same client?

Yes, on the Custom plan: shared agents and apps, roles for owners, editors and viewers, and an audit log of who approved what.

Can we use our own model keys?

Yes. Add your own OpenRouter, Anthropic, OpenAI or Google keys. Work that runs on your own keys doesn't use credits.
