06FAQ

Questions, answered.

More on plans and data in the pricing and security pages.

Pricing →Security →
Do I need to be at my laptop?

No. The laptop needs to be on, but you can send tasks and approve them from your phone through Telegram or the web app.

Can it use my own price list?

Yes. Give Titan your price list as a spreadsheet or PDF and it prices every quote from it.

Does it message customers on its own?

No. Messages go out only after you approve them, and you can edit any of them first.
