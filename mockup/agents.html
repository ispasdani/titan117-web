Agents

Ten specialists. One memory. All of them ask first.

Titan plans the work and hands parts of it to specialist agents. They share what they learn, run on your laptop, and wait for your approval before they act. Every demo below is live: click around.

01 / 10 Titan · permission layer

Titan plans the work. Nothing touches your files until you say yes.

Every file, shell command, website and email goes through one approval layer. Set rules for what is always fine, and give each task a credit budget it can't go past.

  • Approve or deny each action, or set a rule once
  • Budgets cap what a task can spend
  • Web pages are read as data, never as instructions
02 / 10 Model router

Pick the model for each agent. See the cost before it runs.

Run local models for free and offline, or the strongest models through OpenRouter. Use our credits or your own API key, and switch per agent or per step.

  • llama.cpp built in, nothing else to install
  • OpenRouter, Anthropic, OpenAI and Google keys
  • Every task shows its estimate first
03 / 10 Folder agent

Ask your documents. Get the answer and the page it came from.

Point Titan at a folder. It reads PDFs, Word files, spreadsheets and scans, then answers with the file and page number, so you can check every claim in one click.

  • OCR for scanned paperwork
  • Spreadsheet maths done in code, not guessed
  • Mark a folder local-only and it never reaches a cloud model
04 / 10 Code builder

Fixes the bug, runs the tests, opens the pull request.

The coding engine plans, edits, runs your test suite and shows you the diff before anything is committed. Review it like a teammate's work.

  • Plan and act modes
  • Diffs, tests and git in one place
  • Sub-agents for review
05 / 10 App builder

Describe the app. Watch it build, test and publish.

Web, mobile or desktop, one target per project. Preview it on your laptop, test it in a real browser or the Android emulator, then publish or add it to your sidebar.

  • Expo for phones, Tauri for desktop apps
  • Browser tests before every publish
  • Versions you can roll back
06 / 10 Sidebar apps

Your CRM and your stock list, and Titan answers across both.

Apps you build live in the sidebar and share one local database, so a question about customers can draw on inventory, invoices and anything else you keep there.

  • One local database for every app
  • Ask across apps in plain language
  • Build new apps with the app builder
07 / 10 QA and SEO agents

Tests your site like a customer, then fixes what it finds.

The QA agent clicks through every flow and writes the tests. The SEO agent audits pages, keywords and AI visibility, then proposes the fixes as a change you approve.

  • Real-browser tests on every screen size
  • SEO, accessibility and speed audits
  • Fixes arrive as one reviewable change
08 / 10 Automation

Runs on a schedule and watches the web while you work.

Schedule reports, record a task once and replay it, or have a watcher tell you when a law, a price or a competitor's page changes. Missed runs catch up the next time you open the app.

  • Schedules that catch up after sleep
  • Record once, replay on a schedule
  • Watchers for laws, prices and pages
09 / 10 Marketing and video

Turn your website into a promo video and a month of posts.

Titan reads your site and brand kit, cuts a short video with open-source tools, and plans posts for LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and X on one calendar.

  • Videos rendered on your laptop
  • Posts in your brand voice
  • One calendar for every channel
10 / 10 Remote and channels

Start a task from your phone. Approve it from chat.

The web app and Telegram talk to the engine on your laptop with end-to-end encryption. Your laptop does the work; your phone just says yes.

  • Telegram and the web app
  • End-to-end encrypted commands
  • Approvals wherever you are
