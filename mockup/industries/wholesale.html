06FAQ

Questions, answered.

More on plans and data in the pricing and security pages.

Pricing →Security →
Can it read our customers' order formats?

Titan reads PDF, Word, Excel and email orders, scans included, and asks you to confirm anything it is unsure about before entering it.

What happens if the laptop goes offline?

Local work keeps running on local models for up to 14 days on a signed licence. Tasks that need the internet wait in a queue and run when you are back online.

Can it sign in to supplier portals?

It replays tasks you record once, and signing in to a website is always an action you approve first.
