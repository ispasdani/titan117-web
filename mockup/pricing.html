04FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is a credit?

1 credit is €0.01 of cloud usage at our price: the provider's cost plus 25%, rounded up. Every task shows its estimate first and can be capped.

What never uses credits?

Local models, previews, tests, documents, and anything you run with your own API key.

Do unused credits roll over?

Monthly credits roll over for one month, up to one month's allowance. Top-up credits last 12 months while a paid plan is active.

What happens if I cancel?

Your plan runs to the end of the paid period. Then the app locks, monthly credits expire, and top-ups wait up to 12 months for a new subscription. Your files stay on your disk.

Are prices shown with VAT?

Prices are before VAT, which is added at checkout where it applies. We charge in EUR and USD, with the same numbers in both.

Is there a free plan or a trial?

No. TITAN117 works with an active subscription, starting with Starter at €15 a month.
